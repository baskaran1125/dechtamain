## 🎯 WHAT WAS WRONG

The vendorController was calling `otpService.sendOtp()` but the service only exports `generateOtp()`.

This caused a **runtime error → 500 response**.

---

## ✅ WHAT'S NOW FIXED

**File:** `vendorController.js`

**Changes:**
1. Line 35: `otpService.sendOtp()` → `otpService.generateOtp()`
2. Line 42: `result.otp` → `otp`
3. Line 72: `otpResult.success` → `otpResult.valid`
4. Line 75: `otpResult.message` → `otpResult.reason`
